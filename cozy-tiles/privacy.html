Cozy Tiles – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026 · Developer: gamesndroid ("we", "us") · Contact: gamesndroid6@gmail.com

Cozy Tiles is a relaxing tile-matching puzzle game. We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. This policy explains the limited data the game and its service providers handle, and your choices.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, daily puzzle streak, room decorations, hints and shuffles, settings such as sound and colours) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

When you first open the game we ask for your year of birth. It is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. The game uses it to choose suitable ads: players under 16 see only ads suitable for all ages, never personalised, and are not shown the ad consent form.

2. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Remote Config. They may collect:

  • App interactions, such as levels started and completed, hints, shuffles and undos used, shop visits and session length;
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version, language and approximate (country-level) location derived from the IP address;
  • App instance identifiers and, where permitted, the Android advertising ID;
  • Crash logs and diagnostic information.

This information is not used to identify you personally. See Firebase privacy information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address and ad-interaction data to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure their performance and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ads in Cozy Tiles are designed to be respectful: no ads in your first minutes of play, full-screen ads only between levels after a win and always announced, and optional reward videos you choose to watch. Every helper you can buy can also be earned by watching a video, and if no video is available the helper is given free.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy optional items through Google Play: Remove Ads, helper packs (hints and shuffles) and a one-time starter pack. Payments are processed by Google; we never receive your card or payment details. We only learn which item was bought, so the game can deliver it.

5. Children

Cozy Tiles is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. If a player's year of birth shows they are under 16, ads are limited to content suitable for all ages, are not personalised, and are requested as "under the age of consent" (and as child-directed for under-13s). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help delete it.

6. Data sharing and retention

We don't sell your data. Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google), who process it for the purposes described. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change ad consent (EEA/UK/CH) from the consent prompt, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings;
  • Turn off ad personalisation in your Google account or device settings;
  • Ask us to delete data associated with your app instance by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data.

How to delete your data

Cozy Tiles has no accounts. Your game progress lives only on your device: uninstalling the game deletes it. To delete analytics and crash data linked to your copy of the game, email gamesndroid6@gmail.com with the subject "Cozy Tiles data deletion". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Analytics data is otherwise deleted automatically after 14 months and crash reports after 90 days. Advertising data held by Google is controlled from your Google account and device settings (reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings).

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections by Google's services.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions or requests: gamesndroid6@gmail.com
