friendly themed crosswords

  • Big letters and a big keyboard; Large and Extra large text in Settings
  • Six pages make an edition you can reread in the Press Room
  • A free hint on every page, a daily crossword with no ads, and endless puzzles
  • No timer, never an ad in the middle of a puzzle, and it plays offline
