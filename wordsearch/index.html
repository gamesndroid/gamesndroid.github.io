Quilted Words

big letters · a secret in every puzzle · no timer

A cosy word search. Find the words, and the letters left over spell a true fact about the theme. Every finished puzzle becomes a patchwork block, and eight blocks make a quilt for your quilt room.

  • Big, easy letters; Large and Extra large text in Settings
  • Swipe along a word, or tap its first and last letters
  • Eight quilts of themes: Garden, Kitchen, Seaside, Farm, Music, Travel, Sewing Basket, Four Seasons
  • A free hint on every puzzle, a daily puzzle with no ads, and endless puzzles
  • No timer, never an ad in the middle of a puzzle, and it plays offline
